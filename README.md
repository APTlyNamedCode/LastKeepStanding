# Last Keep Standing

An offline-capable, single-player strategy game. Recruit troops, build defenses and advance through four ages to destroy the enemy keep while protecting your own.

## Play

[Play online](https://mackmaton.com/games/last-keep-standing/), or download [index.html](index.html) and open it in a modern web browser.

- No installation, build step or dependencies.
- In-game Help explains the controls and rules.
- Music and sound effects are optional.
- Match progress is not saved.
