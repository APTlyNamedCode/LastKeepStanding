Command the field

1–4 recruit · Space pause · V arm Royal volley · Esc cancel. While aiming, use arrows to move the reticle and Enter to fire. Otherwise, focus the field and use left/right arrows to pan. Drag the field, use a horizontal trackpad gesture, or use the camera slider. Tab navigates controls; Enter activates the focused button. Space remains the match-pause shortcut.

Small windows & volley timing

In taller narrow windows, select Recruit, Fortify, Royal volley or Age above the command panel. With a command tab focused, use left/right arrows or Home/End to switch groups. Short windows keep command groups in a scrollable dock; the workshop instead uses Spot → Design → Confirm pages. An unbuilt spot goes straight to tower confirmation, then to weapon design after construction. Back revisits your choice. Escape closes the workshop and restores its opener.

Royal volley fires three waves from your keep's royal battery at a chosen point, independently of your purchased turrets. Guard Archers, Longbow Salvo, Mangonel Barrage and Royal Cannonade fire 18 / 24 / 9 / 12 projectiles in total. The initial warning is 1.1s; base flight is 2.1 / 2.3 / 2.6 / 2.2s, with wave flight offsets of 0 / +0.08 / −0.04s and launch gaps of 0.42 / 0.38 / 0.55 / 0.6s by age. First impact is at 3.2 / 3.4 / 3.7 / 3.3s; the last arrives at 4 / 4.12 / 4.76 / 4.46s. The HUD tracks the next and last arrival until all waves land. The same total damage is split across three area hits on enemy troops, never keeps; troops can leave the zone between hits. Damage is fixed at launch, even if you advance before impact. The aiming count is live, not a guaranteed hit count. Only aiming can be cancelled, not a called salvo. Royal volley is free and recharges in 45 simulation seconds. Dragging never fires. Pausing stops everything, including income and training.

Mangonels and field cannons advance while fighting troops, then stop when the keep is in range. They keep firing at the keep, even when defenders emerge. Allies can pass this parked siege — but cannot pass it on the road. Both resist 50% of ballista bolt damage; other attacks remain fully effective.

Rams hit keeps for 8× base damage: 240 in Dark Age, 324 in Feudal. Their armour reduces ordinary ranged and turret projectile damage by 60%; melee attacks and Royal volleys still deal full damage. They must reach melee range and attack an eligible nearby defender before resuming attacks on the keep. Ranged siege deals 2× damage to keeps. All siege deals only half base damage to troops.

Five queued recruits train one at a time; 24 deployed troops per side. Except when passing parked ranged siege, allies queue along the same lane; fresh spawns may overlap at the gate until forward space opens. Ordinary ranged troops still stop to fire, so recruitment order matters. At the troop cap, a finished recruit holds its slot until space opens. Recruitment cannot be cancelled. Training times above come from each age's troop stats.

Four distinct arsenals

Dark · Timber & hide

Timber halls, axe-bearing militia, leather shields, slings and an exposed log ram. A simple sling perch guards the gate.

Feudal · Stone & fletching

Stone foundations, quilted armor, spears, bows and covered rams. A second mount and rapid archer towers expand the defense.

Castle · Steel & siege

Masonry battlements, plated infantry, armored knights and advancing mangonels. Three mounts support long-range ballistas.

Renaissance · Powder & brass

Refined bastions, crested helmets, pikes and matchlocks. Field cannons roll forward under fire to break keeps; mounted cannons punish clustered troops.

Damage, upgrades & selling

Range is measured along the lane from each mount. Turrets target troops, never keeps. All ranged damage happens on impact, not launch. Direct shots follow their target and disappear harmlessly if it is lost. Ballista bolts sweep through up to three enemy troops on their lane path, with 60% damage after the first hit. Mangonels and field cannons take half damage from each ballista contact. Rams instead take 40% damage from all ordinary ranged and turret projectiles, including secondary hits; Royal volleys bypass this armour. Cannon shots land at a fixed ground point: enemies can leave the 45-unit blast, and secondary hits deal 50% damage before armour. Secondary turret victims must be in range. Friendly troops are never hit.

Fixed era damage: a design's unlock age sets its damage; advancing does not increase it. Two rank upgrades add +25% each and cost 60% / 90% of that design's purchase price. Selling returns half of weapon purchase + upgrade gold, rounded down, never the tower cost. The structure stays built and can be armed again. Sell first to change designs. Unlocked designs are choices, not automatic additions. New turrets must load a full shot; advancement and upgrades do not reset cooldowns.

Towers and turrets share their keep's survival, not separate health; neither adds keep HP. Wall ranks cost 120/220/360, each adding 15% of the current age's base keep HP while preserving health percentage. Walls, towers and installed turrets persist across ages. Melee strikes resolve immediately; ranged shots and each volley wave resolve on arrival, once only.
